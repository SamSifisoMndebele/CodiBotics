Empowering South African primary schools with a free, 100% offline-capable Coding and Robotics platform aligned with the Department of Basic Education (DBE) CAPS curriculum for Grades R to 7.

DBE CAPS Curriculum Phases (Grades R–7)

Foundation Phase (Grades R–3): Tactile Floor Robot & Spatial Grid Maps

Learn algorithmic sequencing, pattern recognition, and spatial navigation across South African themed 5x5 mats including Township Solar Grid, Kruger Wildlife Safari, and Cape Town Harbour.

Intermediate Phase (Grades 4–6): Blockly Visual Coding, 5x5 Microcontroller & MicroPython

Build programs with Blockly blocks, test on a virtual 5x5 LED matrix microcontroller with temperature, light, button, and pin sensors, and transpile directly to real MicroPython code.

Senior Phase (Grade 7): 2D Autonomous Rover Kinematics & Digital Logic Gates

Program differential-drive autonomous rovers with ultrasonic raycasting and line-following sensors, and design Boolean logic circuits with AND, OR, NOT, XOR, and NAND gates.
